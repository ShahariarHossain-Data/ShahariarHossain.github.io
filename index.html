I’m Md. Shahariar Hossain, a Data Analyst and Power BI Developer based in Dhaka. I work across the whole reporting chain: collecting and cleaning data with SQL, Power Query and Excel, modeling it in Power BI, and presenting it in dashboards that make the next decision obvious.

I started on the business side as a merchandiser, working with international fashion buyers on orders, costing and sales. That is where I learned that a report only matters if someone acts on it, and why I design every dashboard around the question it needs to answer.

I hold a BSc in Mathematics and keep building on it with Python, Snowflake and statistical methods like cohort analysis and forecasting. Right now I’m expanding into Power Automate, AI automation and RPA, so that routine reporting runs by itself and teams can spend their time on analysis.
